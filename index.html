Work Experience

How have I made an impact?

Vision and Graphics Lab, IIT Delhi
Research Associate
Oct 2024 - Jul 2025

  • Conducted research and experimentation under the guidance of Dr. Chetan Arora to improve the detection accuracy for occluded vehicle scenarios in the Indian road context.
  • Evaluated transformer-based detection models (DETR, DINO, Grounding DINO, Co-DETR) on the RSUD20K, IDD and BDD100K datasets to identify model strengths and areas for improvement.
  • Enhanced the RSUD20K dataset to include high occlusion scenarios (>50%) using Grounding DINO pseudo-labeling and manual refinement. Developed a Python-based GUI using Tkinter to efficiently correct false positives, significantly reducing manual annotation time.

Mantra Softech
Research Associate Intern
Dec 2023 - Oct 2024

  • Worked on improving and optimizing the AI pipeline for video surveillance.
  • Conducted in-depth research to identify an optimal deep neural network for people count and recognition, suitable for constrained edge deployment.
  • Employed optimization techniques such as model pruning, quantization, multiprocessing, frame filtering, and batch inference for optimizing resource allocation and accommodating scalability for multiple cameras.
  • Researched and evaluated open-source GenAI models, including DALL-E Mini, Stable Diffusion, CLIP, and various LLMs, to identify an effective solution for retrieving videos from a large database using text prompts.
